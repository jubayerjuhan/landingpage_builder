How to Test

  1. Open the builder at http://localhost:5557/
  2. Add a Section element from the Layout panel
  3. Drag a Video element from the Media panel into the section
  4. Double-click the video placeholder to open the URL editor
  5. Paste any of the URLs above and press Enter or click Save
  6. The video should load and play with the Vidstack player
  7. You can toggle autoplay, controls, muted, and loop in the properties panel
